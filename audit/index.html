Why it matters

Most validation sets come from a handful of scanners.

Training and test data usually share the same hospitals and the same machines, so a strong test score says little about the next site. The gap shows up later, during a deployment or in front of a regulator, when it is expensive to fix. An audit finds it while you can still act.

When teams ask for one

  • Before a submissionEvidence of how the model generalises across sites and scanners.
  • Before a new siteCheck a hospital's scanners before the model goes live there.
  • After a dropPerformance fell somewhere and you need to know why.
  • Before retrainingLearn which scanners and protocols your next round of data should cover.

Why not do it in-house

Running the model is the easy part.

Any capable engineer, or an AI coding assistant, can run a model over a folder of scans and print a number for each scanner. Whether that number means anything depends on everything around it.

What you get

Answers you can act on, not one pooled score.

Every result is broken down so your team can see which scanners are the problem and what to do about them.

  • Performance by scannerBroken down by vendor, field strength and protocol. On public data this measures consistency: whether the same brain gets the same result on every scanner. With your own labelled data, accuracy too.
  • Reference comparisonWhere your model sits against widely used open models, scanner by scanner.
  • The failure casesThe individual scans where the model goes wrong, with the images, so your team can look.
  • Likely physical causesBias field, contrast, resolution, reconstruction. We say which ones are driving the shift.
  • RecommendationsWhat to change: data to collect, preprocessing to add, sites to watch.
  • A written reportClear enough to hand to your clinical or regulatory lead as it is, plus a recorded walkthrough.

How it runs

What we need from you

Less than you might think.

We do not need your training data, and your model does not have to leave your control.

  • The taskWhat it predicts and the metric you report it on.

Confidentiality

Your model and your data stay yours.

Every audit runs under an NDA. We do not keep your model, your data or anything derived from your own scans once the work is finished.

With your written permission, we keep results from the public-dataset part of an audit, anonymised, for our own research. We never publish anything that identifies you or your model, and you can say no.
