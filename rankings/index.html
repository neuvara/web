What the numbers mean

One brain, many scanners, one answer.

Each person in the test set is scanned on every scanner, so their anatomy is identical throughout. Any change in a model's output from one scanner to the next comes from the scanner, not the brain.

  • Cross-scanner variationHow much a structure's measured volume changes for the same person across scanners. Models are ranked on this. Lower is better.
  • AgreementHow closely the segmentations of the same brain match between scanners, as a Dice score where 1 means identical. Higher is better.
  • Worst scannerThe largest average offset on any single scanner, as a share of the person's average volume. Shows whether one scanner is a problem.
  • ScannersHow many scanners the model was tested on.
