Privacy policy

Last updated 5 October 2026

Who we are

Neuvara is a UK-based company providing MRI engineering and analysis services to companies and research groups. This policy covers this website, enquiries you send us, and what we keep from client work.

Do not send patient information

Please do not send patient information, medical records, imaging files, clinical notes or anything that could identify a patient through this website or by email. Where a project involves imaging data, we agree how it is handled in writing first, and we work inside the client's environment or on data they have already de-identified.

The contact form

When you use the contact form we receive your name, email address, organisation (if you give one), the topic you choose and your message. The form is delivered to our inbox by Web3Forms, a form-delivery service.

We use these details only to reply to you, to scope any work, and to keep ordinary business records. We do not sell or share them, and we do not add you to a mailing list.

Email

If you email us directly, we use your contact details and message in the same way: to reply, to scope work, and for business records.

Client work and research

Every engagement runs under a written agreement. When it ends, we do not keep the client's data, model, or anything derived from the client's own scans.

Where a client agrees in writing, we may keep results from the part of an audit that runs on public datasets, such as performance figures and error analyses, and use them in our own research. These results are anonymised. We never publish anything that identifies a client or their model, and clients can decline.

Cookies and analytics

This website sets no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. If we add analytics, this page will be updated first.

How long we keep things

We keep enquiries for as long as needed to respond and for ordinary business records, then delete them. You can ask us to delete yours at any time.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted. Use the contact form or write to contact@neuvara.org. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Not a clinical service

Neuvara does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and does not supply medical devices.
