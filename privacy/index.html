No patient information

Do not submit patient information, protected health information, medical records, imaging files, clinical notes, or other confidential medical data through this website or by email.

Neuvara is a research-stage company. This website describes intended research and validation directions. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or a regulated clinical service, and Neuvara is not a medical device.

Contact by email

This website has no contact form and collects no data. Contact links open your own email client. If you email us, the business contact details and message you send are used only to respond to the enquiry, evaluate fit, and keep ordinary business records.

Cookies and analytics

This website sets no analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies. If analytics are introduced later, this policy will be updated before deployment and configured to avoid collecting patient information.

Privacy contact

For privacy questions, email contact@neuvara.org. Neuvara will publish a monitored privacy contact address before adding analytics, form submission, or any personal data collection.
